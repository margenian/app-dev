# About my favorite movie
***Resident Evil*** is a 2026 horror film directed by Zach Cregger and written by Cregger and Shay Hatten. It is the eighth live-action film in the ***Resident Evil*** film series, based on the video game series by Capcom. Austin Abrams stars as Bryan Hodukavich, a medical courier who must fight through an outbreak of infected monsters in Raccoon City. Rather than acting like an elite soldier or super-cop, Bryan is completely untrained, panicked, and forced to figure out enemy vulnerabilities on the fly while managing limited resources, keys, inventory, and environment puzzles reminiscent of playing a survival-horror video game. You can check the reviews through [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/resident_evil_2026)
